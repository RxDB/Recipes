Apple Milkshake

Ingredients

  • 6 ice apple
  • 500 ml milk
  • 1/2 cup sabja seeds soaked
  • 4 tbsp sugar
  • 1/4 tsp Cardamom powder
  • Dry fruit for decoration

Steps to prepare

  1. Peel wash and cut Ice apple
  2. In mixer jar put pieces of ice apple and sugar. Make fine paste.
  3. Take serving glass put ice apple paste add soaked sabja,cold milk, cardamom powder, small pieces of ice apple,ice cube.
  4. Sprinkle dry fruit and serve.
